How POCKET PATROL handles information.

On this page On your device Leaderboard data Purpose and external services Website analytics Retention and deletion

Last updated: November 5, 2026

01

On your device

Game progress and settings are stored on your device.

02

Leaderboard data

Leaderboards use Google Play Games or Game Center to process player identifiers, profiles, scores and gameplay records. Player names and scores are displayed according to each service’s visibility settings. Google Play Games also processes usage and diagnostic information to improve service quality.

03

Purpose and external services

Google Play Games and Game Center provide leaderboards and authentication. Older versions use Firebase. These services process information to provide their features and maintain security.

Firebase Privacy and Security · Google Privacy Policy · Game Center & Privacy

Advertising uses Google AdMob. Its SDK collects and shares IP addresses (including approximate location), device and related identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent is requested where required, and applicable choices can be changed in the app.

04

Website analytics

This official website loads Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) and uses analytics cookies only if you allow it. Page visits, link interactions, referrers, device/browser information and approximate region are sent to Google to understand and improve website use. Your IP address is provided to Google when connecting. Google signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled for this web measurement, and it does not send your in-game account information.

Before you allow analytics, or if you choose “Do not allow,” this site does not load its Google Analytics tag or create analytics cookies. Your language and cookie choice are stored in your browser. Cookie choices and analytics cookies last for up to 180 days. Withdrawing permission in the footer’s “Cookie settings” deletes the analytics cookies set by this site and stops subsequent measurement. Previously sent records remain subject to Google’s policies and this service’s retention settings.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites that use its services

05

Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service. Records held by external services are managed under their policies. For deletion, use the relevant service’s settings or contact us through the X link in the footer.
