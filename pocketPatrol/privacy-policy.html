01

On your device

Progress, settings, collection records and local scores are stored on your device. Apart from leaderboard submissions, these records are not sent to a server for ordinary gameplay.

02

Leaderboard data

If you choose to submit a national leaderboard score, we send an anonymous Firebase user ID, score, optional display name (up to eight characters), optional manually selected Japanese region, stage, difficulty, craft, clear/game-over result, submission time and run ID. Guest credentials are created and stored on your device for secure writes. There is no email/password account registration.

Display names, selected regions, scores, stages, difficulties, crafts, results and submission times are public. Anonymous IDs and run IDs are also included in downloadable records. Do not use your real name or precise address as a display name. We do not obtain regions from GPS. Leaderboard submission is optional; you can play without submitting.

03

Purpose and external services

We use this data to provide the leaderboard, prevent duplicate abuse, authenticate submissions and protect the service. Data is sent over HTTPS to Google Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database. Google may process IP addresses and related information for security.

Firebase Privacy and Security · Google Privacy Policy

This release includes no advertising SDK, advertising ID access, in-app purchases or behavioral analytics SDK. We do not sell your data.

04

Retention and deletion

The public leaderboard retains the top 1,000 scores. Records may be replaced or removed during periodic maintenance; we cannot guarantee when an individual record will leave the board. Guest identities may be retained until deletion is requested or they are no longer needed. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records but does not automatically delete server data.
