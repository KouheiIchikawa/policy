01

On your device

Progress, settings, collection records and local scores are stored on your device. Apart from leaderboard submissions, these records are not sent to a server for ordinary gameplay.

02

Leaderboard data

Android 1.0.51 and later use Google Play Games; the upcoming iPhone version uses Game Center. Participating authenticates with that provider and sends your settled Clear/Game Over score. The SDK processes player identifiers, profiles and connection information under the provider’s policies. The Google Play Games SDK also collects usage analytics and diagnostic information to improve service stability and quality. Rankings are separate by platform; campaign progress is not synced across devices. Versions up to 1.0.50 used Firebase guest IDs, scores, optional names of up to eight characters, selected regions, stages, difficulty, craft, results, times and run IDs. That legacy service is being retired; its records are not automatically moved to the new boards.

New boards show the platform player name/profile and score according to profile visibility settings. There is no separate eight-character name or region form. Legacy Firebase records publicly included name, region, score, stage, difficulty, craft, result and time, with anonymous identifiers in the distributed data. GPS is not used. You can play without using leaderboards.

03

Purpose and external services

Leaderboards and authentication use Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. These providers process information to deliver and secure their services. Legacy-version compatibility uses Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database. Connections are encrypted. Please also review Google’s and Apple’s privacy policies.

Firebase Privacy and Security · Google Privacy Policy · Game Center & Privacy

Ad-supported versions 1.0.52 and later show Google AdMob interstitial ads between stages and on Continue. Google’s advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (including approximate location inference), device/account identifiers, ad interactions, advertising data, usage and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google UMP requests consent where required; when applicable, choices can be changed from the in-game Privacy screen. Android verification APKs display test ads. Versions through 1.0.51 do not include the advertising SDK. There are no in-app purchases.

04

Retention and deletion

New leaderboard retention, visibility and deletion follow each platform’s policies and settings. The old Firebase top-1,000 storage cap does not apply to the new services. Legacy records are retained for compatibility and deletion requests and will be retired with that service. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data, not automatically cloud records.
