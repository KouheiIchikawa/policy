Privacy policy

How POCKET PATROL handles information.

On this page On your device Leaderboard data Purpose and external services Retention and deletion

Last updated: November 1, 2026

01

On your device

Game progress and settings are stored on your device.

02

Leaderboard data

Leaderboards use Google Play Games or Game Center to process player identifiers, profiles, scores and gameplay records. Player names and scores are displayed according to each service’s visibility settings. Google Play Games also processes usage and diagnostic information to improve service quality.

03

Purpose and external services

Google Play Games and Game Center provide leaderboards and authentication. Older versions use Firebase. These services process information to provide their features and maintain security.

Firebase Privacy and Security · Google Privacy Policy · Game Center & Privacy

Advertising uses Google AdMob. Its SDK collects and shares IP addresses (including approximate location), device and related identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent is requested where required, and applicable choices can be changed in the app.

04

Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service. Records held by external services are managed under their policies. For deletion, use the relevant service’s settings or contact us through the X link in the footer.
