01 / PARTNERS

同じ旅、違う戦い方。

貫通、3方向、ホーミング。ジャンプ、敵弾消去、時間停止。機体ごとの得意を使って切り抜けよう。

02 / CAMPAIGN

9つの地域を越えて。

氷、炎、海、幻想の世界。前半戦から中ボス、後半戦からボスへ。場所と相手が変われば、攻略も変わる。

BEYOND THE BOUNDARY

Small machines.
A world worth fighting for.

Choose Volt, Flare or Leaf. Travel across nine regions in a story-driven portrait shooter.

Automatic fire lets you focus on movement and Arts. Pierce enemy lines, spread fire across the field, or track targets with homing shots. Face mid-bosses and bosses, discover their stories and build your collection through battle.

Three difficulties, branching endings, optional top-1,000 national scores, and short arcade intermissions. Japanese and English in-game text. Android closed testing is available to invited testers. Update 1.0.48 has been submitted for review; iOS is in development. App price: free. Test availability depends on review and tester invitation.
