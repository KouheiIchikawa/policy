POCKET PATROL / YOUR DATA

Your data. Your control.

Take control of
your play records.

New records are managed by Google Play Games or Game Center. Google provides game-data deletion controls. Contact us on X for help with Game Center records or legacy Firebase records. Uninstalling alone does not erase cloud records.

01

Choose what to remove

Google Play Games profile settings let you delete data for a specific game. For Game Center or legacy Firebase scores/guest identities, tell us which records you want removed. You do not need to erase local campaign progress.

02

Contact us on X

Contact @sio_sio_bull about deleting POCKET PATROL data. We will arrange a private way to exchange the information needed to identify your records.

03

Verify and remove

After ownership verification, we normally remove requested legacy Firebase records within 30 days. Google/Apple-managed records follow each provider’s deletion controls and permissions; we cannot delete your entire platform account on your behalf. We will contact you if further verification or time is needed.

Keep private details private.

For new boards, identify the platform, player name, score and date. Legacy ranking IDs are shown in Settings → Privacy in older builds. These details alone do not prove ownership. Do not publish IDs or private information, and never send passwords or authentication tokens.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and prevent abuse. X messages follow X’s policies; provider records, backups and security logs follow Google’s or Apple’s applicable policies.
