Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy covers BLOOD BLOSSOM for Android and iOS and this official website, provided by Kohei Elimi Lab. Contact repa.le.das@gmail.com.

Game data stored on your device

Story and side-mission progress, experience, in-game currency, tools, materials, ingredients, mission records, locally created Meika stages and results, language and control preferences are saved on your device. Automatic transmission of these saves to the developer is not enabled in the current version.

Meika currently supports local stage creation and play. Online publishing, downloading other users’ stages, cloud synchronization and account registration are not currently offered.

Additional content and platform services

The Google Play edition can download additional game content, including movies, through Google Play delivery services and store it in app-specific storage. Google and Apple may process device and connection information for distribution, updates and operation under their own policies. The developer does not ask for your name or email address for this delivery process.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

The current game includes no advertising SDK, advertising-ID tracking, developer-operated usage analytics SDK or automatic crash-reporting SDK. Diagnostics and purchase information handled by Apple or Google under OS or store settings are subject to their policies.

The game does not collect GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone information. Transactions using in-game money or materials are not additional real-money purchases.

Website access

This is a static GitHub Pages website, without developer-added analytics tags, advertisements, contact forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process IP addresses and other connection information to provide and protect its service. External websites have their own policies.

Support information and retention

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to respond and investigate the issue. Game data is not attached automatically. Do not send unnecessary personal information or a complete save.

We retain information only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion.

Deleting local data

Use the game’s Settings and confirm data initialization to reset the selected game progress and records. To remove all app data, including preferences, use Android app storage controls or Delete App on iOS. Offload App on iOS may retain data.

The developer cannot remotely restore deleted progress. Backup and restoration also depend on your device and OS settings.

Updates and related policies

We update this page and store declarations when functionality or data handling changes.
