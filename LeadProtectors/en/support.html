Support

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Contact support

Email repa.le.das@gmail.com about bugs, controls or purchases. If possible, include the app version, device model, OS, what happened and steps to reproduce it. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Can I transfer my save?

Saves are currently local only. There is no account or cloud transfer. Take care before uninstalling the app or clearing device data.

Do I need an internet connection?

Built-in courses do not require a permanent connection. Downloading extra courses and opening external policy pages require internet access.

How do I stop riding?

Open the map with ≡ at the top right and choose to end the outing. Exit may be unavailable during falls or incidents. Unfinished deliveries do not earn pay.

Motion, haptics and language

Adjust them under Settings → Display & Haptics. Riding modes are under Settings → Riding. Use Rest to advance a week when energy is low.

Refunds

Use the refund procedure in the App Store or Google Play purchase history. If you send an order number to support, hide unrelated personal details.
