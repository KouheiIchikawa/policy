Privacy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy applies to LEAD PROTECTORS (リードプロテクターズ) and this official website, provided by Kohei Elimi Lab. Contact: repa.le.das@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, character appearance and age choices, in-game funds, vehicles and gear, the game calendar and weather, delivery and school records, language/display/haptic settings and optional ride replays are saved locally. The age is a fictional character setting. The developer does not automatically upload these files. There is currently no account registration, cloud save or cross-device sync.

Device sensors

Accelerometer, gravity and gyroscope readings control riding, and haptics provide feedback. Readings are processed in the game and are not sent to developer servers. The game does not collect or use GPS location, contacts, the camera or the microphone. Ride replays record game motion, not camera footage or microphone audio.

Additional course downloads

Course listings and data are requested over HTTPS from Google Firebase Hosting at gd-leadprotectors.web.app. These requests do not include your name, email, local saves or sensor readings. The service provider may process IP addresses and standard connection information for delivery and security. Downloaded courses are cached locally. See Google’s Privacy Policy for its processing.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

There is no advertising SDK, advertising identifier, behavioural tracking, in-app analytics SDK or automatic crash upload to the developer. Apple and Google may process purchases or diagnostics under their own platform policies and your settings. Purchases within gameplay use fictional currency, not additional real-money payments.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics tags, advertising, tracking cookies or contact forms. Language selection navigates between Japanese and English URLs. GitHub may process connection information to operate and protect its services. External websites have their own policies.

Support and retention

If you email us, we use your address, message and any information you choose to attach to answer and investigate. Game data is never attached automatically. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for support or legal obligations, then delete unnecessary information. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion.

Deleting saves and changes

After starting from the title, Settings → Riding → Clear data resets game progress. To delete all local app data, including remaining course caches, clear app storage in Android settings or use Delete App on iOS. Offload App on iOS retains data. Deleted progress cannot be recovered by the developer, who cannot remotely access your save. We update this page when features or information practices change.
