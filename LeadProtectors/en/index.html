YOUR FIRST MOTORCYCLE LIFE

Your licence is just the beginning.

Start with the basics at riding school. Turn the key, start the engine, and head into town. Work, shop, or take a ride. Your week is yours to choose.

01 / LEARN

Your first steps, at riding school.

Practise starts, braking and junction turns with your instructor. Choose Normal or Hard and learn at your own pace.

02 / WORK

A job on two wheels.

Take 500 m, 1 km or 2 km delivery jobs. Meet the customer at the destination and earn your pay. Start small and build confidence.

03 / LIVE

One bike. A life of your own.

Visit Dream Plaza for a new machine, colours and gear. Wash your bike when it gets dirty. Plan your weeks around energy and the weather.

04 / RIDE

Where will Sunday take you?

Go touring on your own motorcycle. Explore different riding styles, engine sounds and rider positions as the road unfolds.

FIND YOUR OWN RHYTHM

Make a machine your own.

Scooters, roadsters, sports bikes and cruisers. Choose your colours and gear, and spend time with your favourite machine.
